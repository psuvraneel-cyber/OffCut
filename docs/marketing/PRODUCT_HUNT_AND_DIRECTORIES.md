### 💬 First Maker Comment (Post immediately after launch)

```markdown
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm the creator of **Offcut**, and I'm building an open-source, in-browser alternative to OpusClip.

### Full Transparency First
I want to be completely honest with the PH community: Offcut is an active build-in-public project. The full automated video clipper is in development, but today I am releasing **Phase 1: our fully functional in-browser AI captioning & transcription tool**.

### Why build an open-source OpusClip?
Tools like OpusClip are amazing for turning long podcasts into vertical clips, but almost every existing solution forces you to:
1. Upload multi-gigabyte video files to remote cloud servers.
2. Wait in long processing queues.
3. Pay $20–$40/month just to export clips without watermarks.
4. Give up control and privacy of your raw footage.

With modern WebGPU and Transformers.js, consumer laptops (Apple Silicon, RTX cards, even modern integrated graphics) can run speech models locally in real-time.

### What is live and ready to use today (Phase 1):
- 🛡️ **0 Bytes Uploaded:** Drop in any video file. Transcription runs 100% locally on your computer's GPU using quantized OpenAI Whisper models via WebGPU.
- ⚡ **Instant Word-Level Captions:** Precise timestamps and instant `.srt` and `.vtt` exports for your editing workflow.
- 💸 **100% Free & Open Source:** Apache 2.0 license. Zero subscriptions, zero credits, zero watermarks.
- 🪶 **$0 Server Costs:** Because all heavy tensor computing runs on your own device, Offcut costs nothing to host, ensuring it remains free forever.

### The Roadmap (What's being built next):
- Step 2: Audio energy & sentence-boundary hook scoring to suggest viral 30–60s clips.
- Step 3: Face detection and auto-centering for 9:16 vertical shorts.
- Step 4: Kinetic animated captions rendered directly via WebCodecs.

### Tech Stack Under the Hood
- `@huggingface/transformers` (Transformers.js v3) running ONNX Runtime WebGPU
- `mediabunny` for fast client-side audio demuxing without heavy FFmpeg WASM bloat
- React 19 + Vite + Cloudflare Pages

Try the live caption tool right now (no signup required):
👉 https://offcut-ai.pages.dev

Source code is 100% open source under Apache 2.0:
👉 https://github.com/psuvraneel-cyber/offcut

I would love your feedback on the captioning performance across your devices, and suggestions for the clipping roadmap!
```
